Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Store from the coin badge on the home screen and tap Restore purchases. The story pack and ad-free purchases are restored to the account signed in to the App Store. Coin packs are consumed when bought and cannot be restored.

My streak reset. Why?

A streak counts consecutive days on which you finish the daily puzzle. Missing a day resets it. If you have missed exactly one day, Streak insurance in the Store restores the streak for in-game coins.

How do I report a bug?

Email the address above. Please include your device model and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot helps.
